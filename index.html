Expert Guidance

WHEN SHOULD YOU CONSIDER A VEHICLE INSPECTION?

Look out for these general signs indicating your car may need professional attention.

Unusual sounds +

Squealing, grinding, knocking, or rattling sounds can indicate component wear or looseness.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Warning lights +

Check engine, oil pressure, or battery warning symbols on the dashboard.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Starting problems +

Difficulty turning over the engine or clicking sounds when turning the key.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Brake concerns +

Soft brake pedal, vibration while braking, or reduced stopping responsiveness.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Tire issues +

Uneven tread wear, constant pressure loss, or steering pull.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

AC performance changes +

Reduced cooling capacity or musty odors when operating the air conditioner.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Electrical issues +

Flickering headlights, malfunctioning power windows, or erratic instrument gauges.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

Routine maintenance +

Mileage milestone reached for oil change, fluid flush, or filter replacement.

Recommended next step: professional inspection.

For safety-critical concerns, seek professional inspection rather than attempting repairs yourself.
